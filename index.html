Artist Profile

Adeyemi Temilolu

Born in Lagos and originally from Ijebu in Ogun State, Adeyemi Temilolu has lived most of his life in Lagos. He studied art at the University of Lagos and completed his industrial attachment at Triad Studio, Obalende. He graduated in 2018.

He approaches every canvas with an open mind and an intentional approach. In his work, black depicts the strength of the mind. He employs a mix of charcoal on board and acrylic medium on canvas, using a limited range of black and grey values as opposed to a full spectrum of colour — both mediums blended finely so the figures appear real, yet surreal, with metaphysical body features.

His output is a constant stream of colourful, lifelike figures: clearly secluded images that capture flashes of deep emotions, extending the intent of his feelings and experiences into unceasing ideas.

Beyond the conspicuous

Glamorous wings, robotic body parts and, in some cases, animal prints appear on a simple nude or semi-nude being. Some of his subjects appear quite beautiful; others more frightening, questioning the reality of general perception. They are proposed to represent the constrained power in both men and women.

Adeyemi began his creative path in order to encourage the individuals who view his work — inviting them to investigate the peculiar forces surrounding them, and to appreciate the beauty of growth. His aim is to create awareness that makes his audience see beyond the obstruction of the conspicuous.

The concentrated segment of his painted figures is a fragment of conceptualisation — an art movement that emerged between the 1960s and 1970s to comprehend concepts that could not be effortlessly understood.
